OUR STORY / PEOPLE. PURPOSE. PROGRESS.

One shared vision.
Global possibilities.

Built on hospitality experience. Driven by genuine relationships.

KOONAFA began in Dubai when a group of hospitality professionals, industry friends and Hotel Management graduates came together around one idea: great travel brands deserve stronger connections to the people who sell them.

What started with shared industry knowledge and trusted trade relationships grew into a hospitality marketing and representation company focused on helping hotels, resorts, DMCs and destinations enter new markets with confidence.

Our mission: make global growth more personal, more strategic and more measurable—through local insight, purposeful partnerships and disciplined commercial execution.
