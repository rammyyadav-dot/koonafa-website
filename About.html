OUR STORY / BUILT FOR COMMERCIAL GROWTH

Market presence.
Measurable momentum.

At Koonafa, we build and scale world-class hospitality, travel and tourism brands. We are a specialized marketing, sales representation, DMC representation and commercial growth agency designed to turn market presence into measurable, high-yielding revenue.

Led by a powerhouse team of Hotel Management graduates (IHM) and industry veterans, we operate at the precise intersection of destination expertise, travel trade networking, commercial strategy and market intelligence.

Whether partnering with global DMCs, hotels, tourism boards or travel tech innovators, Koonafa provides the high-impact representation and market access required to scale seamlessly across regions.
