FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

UAE hospitality representation explained.

What does a UAE hospitality representation company do?

It helps hospitality suppliers build market visibility, reach qualified buyers, develop relationships with travel partners, and support commercial sales activity. The exact remit depends on each supplier's objectives, products and target markets.

Does Koonafa work with hotels and resorts in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?

Koonafa's UAE proposition is designed for hotels, resorts and hospitality suppliers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the other emirates. Representation scope and market priorities are discussed individually.

Can overseas DMCs market their UAE products through Koonafa?

Yes. The UAE service proposition includes market development and trade engagement for DMCs and destination products, subject to a mutually agreed representation arrangement.

Do you provide guaranteed bookings or revenue?

No. Trade representation and marketing create commercial opportunities, but results depend on product-market fit, contracting, pricing, availability, competitive conditions and execution.

Which markets can you target for UAE hospitality partners?

Koonafa evaluates outreach plans around the supplier's objectives, with a focus on relevant B2B travel trade and source markets. A specific country-level coverage plan is agreed before activity begins.

How can a UAE supplier discuss representation?

Email Hello@koonafa.com with the property or destination portfolio, markets of interest, target segments and desired scope of work. Koonafa can then discuss a proposed engagement.
