DUBAI HOTEL MARKETING / FAQ

Your questions, answered.

What does a hospitality marketing agency do for Dubai hotels?

A hospitality marketing agency helps define hotel positioning and reach relevant guests through search visibility, digital campaigns, content, website conversion work and—where agreed—B2B commercial activity.

Can Koonafa help improve Google visibility for my hotel?

Services may include technical and content SEO, location-relevant hotel landing pages and Google Business Profile optimization for eligible properties. Rankings and local placement are not guaranteed.

Do you also support OTA and bedbank listings?

OTA, bedbank and e-commerce listing assessments can be scoped separately, including content, positioning, promotion consistency and distribution workflows.

Can you support both direct bookings and travel-trade sales?

Yes. Direct channel marketing and B2B buyer development can be planned together while keeping pricing, distribution and partner responsibilities clear.

How soon will hotel marketing produce results?

Timelines depend on the channel, current website, demand, competition, budget and implementation scope. Paid campaigns and SEO often follow different learning and improvement cycles.

Which locations does Koonafa serve?

Koonafa's UAE proposition covers Dubai, Abu Dhabi and other emirates, with an engagement scope tailored to property location, segment and market objectives.
