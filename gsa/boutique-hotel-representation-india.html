Frequently asked
questions.

What does a hotel representation company do in India?

It acts as your dedicated sales office in India — building travel agent relationships, driving trade bookings, running fam trips and marketing your property to the outbound market.

Which hotel types do you represent?

Luxury resorts, city hotels, boutique properties, villa collections and hotel groups seeking Indian outbound demand.

How do Indian travel agents book represented hotels?

Through our trade desk, preferred wholesale partners, bedbank channels and direct hotel contracting supported by our sales team.

Why is India important for international hotels?

India is among the fastest-growing outbound travel markets, with rising luxury, MICE and wedding demand to destinations worldwide.
