QUESTIONS / INDIA GSA

Frequently asked
questions.

What does a General Sales Agent (GSA) do in India?

A GSA represents a travel or hospitality brand in a defined market, supporting trade sales, distribution relationships, marketing activation and commercial growth under an agreed scope.

Which travel businesses can work with Koonafa Global?

Hotels, resorts, DMCs, airlines, cruise lines, bedbanks, attractions, corporate travel brands, mobility suppliers and destination marketing organizations.

Can an overseas DMC appoint an India representation partner?

Yes. An India representation partnership can help overseas DMCs build agent relationships, promote their destination products and coordinate travel trade sales activity.

Does GSA representation include regional trade activity?

Programs can be tailored by market, buyer segment and objective, including major metro markets and selected emerging outbound travel markets.
