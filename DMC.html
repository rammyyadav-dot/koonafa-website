What does a DMC representation company in India do?

It represents an overseas destination management company within the Indian market. The agreed scope can include trade sales, account development, product training, distribution support and commercial reporting.

Why appoint a local DMC sales representative?

A local representative provides market familiarity, buyer development and consistent follow-up during Indian business hours. This supports market entry without the need to establish and manage a separate local office.

Can Koonafa help an overseas DMC find Indian travel-agent partners?

Yes. An engagement can include identifying relevant outbound agencies, presenting your products, developing relationships and supporting agreed partner-onboarding activities.

Do you support DMC sales in Mumbai and Delhi NCR?

Mumbai and Delhi NCR can form part of a targeted India coverage plan. The agreed scope determines account priorities, local meetings and wider regional activity.

How are commercial agreements structured?

Scope, territory, responsibilities, reporting and fees are agreed before work begins. Where appropriate, proposals can include a retainer and performance-linked incentives with defined attribution and payment terms.

How do you measure success?

Through an agreed combination of qualified accounts, activated partners, booking conversion, incremental contribution and repeat business. Meetings and introductions are activity measures; commercial outcomes are assessed separately.

How quickly will representation generate bookings?

Timing depends on destination demand, product competitiveness, seasonality and buyer sales cycles. Initial work focuses on market readiness and account activation, with expectations established against an agreed baseline.
